# P-004 Fraud Indicators and Escalation

## 4.1 Escalation triggers
Escalate to the fraud team rather than resolving as a routine dispute when any of
the following apply: three or more disputed transactions on one account within
thirty days, a disputed transaction in a country where the member has no travel
history, a card present transaction disputed as unauthorized, or any dispute
following a recent credential reset.

## 4.2 Account protection
Where fraud is suspected the card is blocked and reissued before any credit is
considered. The member is notified by their registered channel within one business
day.

## 4.3 Interaction with provisional credit
A fraud escalation does not pause the provisional credit clock in P-001 section
1.3. Both proceed in parallel.
