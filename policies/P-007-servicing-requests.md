# P-007 Servicing Request and Error Resolution Timing

## 7.1 Acknowledgement
A written servicing request or asserted error is acknowledged within five business
days of receipt, excluding weekends and public holidays.

## 7.2 Resolution
The request is resolved, or the reason for declining it explained, within thirty
business days. One extension of up to fifteen business days is permitted where the
borrower is notified in writing before the original period expires, with the reason.

## 7.3 Charges during review
No late fee or negative credit reporting arises from the disputed amount while a
request is under review.

## 7.4 Escalation
Requests involving a potential compliance breach, including a missed notice under
P-006 section 6.2, are escalated to the servicing compliance team rather than closed
by the analyst handling them.
