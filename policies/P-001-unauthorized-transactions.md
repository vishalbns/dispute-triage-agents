# P-001 Unauthorized Electronic Transactions

## 1.1 Scope
Covers member claims that an electronic fund transfer was not authorized by them,
including debit card transactions, ACH debits and online transfers.

## 1.2 Reporting windows and member liability
A member who reports an unauthorized transfer within two business days of learning
of it is liable for no more than $50. Reported after two business days but within
sixty days of the statement being sent, liability rises to no more than $500.
After sixty days from the statement date, the member may be liable for the full
amount, and the claim is referred to a senior reviewer rather than denied outright.

## 1.3 Investigation timing
The institution must complete its investigation within ten business days of
receiving the claim. If the investigation cannot be completed within ten business
days, provisional credit must be issued and the investigation may extend to
forty-five calendar days.

## 1.4 Provisional credit
Provisional credit is issued for the disputed amount plus any related fees. It is
issued only after the member's identity is verified and the transaction is
confirmed as posted. Provisional credit always requires human approval regardless
of amount.
