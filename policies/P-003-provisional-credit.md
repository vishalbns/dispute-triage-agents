# P-003 Provisional Credit and Approval Authority

## 3.1 Approval authority
All provisional credit requires approval by an authorised reviewer before funds
move. No automated system, including any AI assisted system, may release funds
without a recorded human decision.

## 3.2 Thresholds
Credits up to $500 may be approved by a dispute analyst. Credits above $500 and up
to $5,000 require a senior analyst. Above $5,000 requires a team lead and a written
rationale on the case record.

## 3.3 Reversal
If the investigation concludes the transaction was authorised, provisional credit
is reversed with at least five business days of written notice to the member.

## 3.4 Record keeping
Every decision records the reviewer identity, the timestamp, the policy sections
relied upon, and the rationale. Records are retained for seven years.
