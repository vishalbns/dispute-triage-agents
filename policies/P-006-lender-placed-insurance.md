# P-006 Lender-Placed Insurance and Insurance Tracking

## 6.1 Scope
Covers borrower disputes about lender-placed (force-placed) hazard insurance charged
to an escrow account after the tracking system recorded a lapse in the borrower's own
coverage.

## 6.2 Notice requirements before charging
A first written notice is sent at least forty-five days before any lender-placed
premium is charged. A second reminder notice is sent at least thirty days after the
first and at least fifteen days before the charge. If either notice was not sent on
schedule, the charge is invalid and must be reversed in full regardless of coverage
status.

## 6.3 Evidence of continuous coverage
Where the borrower provides evidence that acceptable coverage was in force for all or
part of the lender-placed period, the lender-placed policy is cancelled within fifteen
days of receipt and the premium for the overlapping period is refunded to the escrow
account.

## 6.4 Acceptable evidence
A declarations page showing the property address, carrier, policy number, effective
dates and dwelling coverage amount. A payment receipt alone is not sufficient. Where
evidence is incomplete the borrower is told specifically what is missing rather than
being refused.

## 6.5 Escrow effects
Refunded premium is credited to escrow, and any resulting escrow shortage or surplus
is handled at the next annual analysis unless the borrower asks for an earlier review.
