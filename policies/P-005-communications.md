# P-005 Member Communications Standards

## 5.1 Tone and content
Communications state what will happen next and by when. They avoid admitting
liability on behalf of the institution or the merchant before an investigation
concludes.

## 5.2 Required elements
Every dispute acknowledgement states the case reference, the disputed amount, the
expected decision date, and the member's right to request the documents relied on.

## 5.3 Prohibited content
Communications must not include full card numbers, full account numbers, or any
other person's details. Card references are limited to the last four digits.

## 5.4 Denials
A denial explains the specific policy basis, the evidence considered, and how the
member may appeal, including the appeal window of sixty days.
