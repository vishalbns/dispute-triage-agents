# P-002 Merchant and Service Disputes

## 2.1 Scope
Covers claims where the member authorized the transaction but disputes the goods
or services: item not received, item not as described, duplicate charge, or a
recurring charge after cancellation.

## 2.2 Prerequisite: contact the merchant first
For goods or services disputes the member must first attempt resolution with the
merchant, except where the merchant is unreachable or has ceased trading. The
attempt, including date and outcome, must be recorded on the case.

## 2.3 Chargeback window
Chargebacks must be raised within 120 calendar days of the transaction date, or
within 120 days of the expected delivery date where goods were not received,
subject to a maximum of 540 days from the transaction date.

## 2.4 Duplicate charges
Where the same merchant, amount and card are charged twice within a 24 hour window
and the merchant has not reversed one, the duplicate is treated as a processing
error and resolved without requiring prior merchant contact.
